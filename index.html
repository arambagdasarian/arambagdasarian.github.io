I am a senior at Harvard University studying Statistics and Government, with an expected graduation date of May 2027. My interests include causal inference, partial identification, and the evaluation of language models.

I am interested in how we can draw reliable conclusions when language models are used to measure social phenomena and their errors change across settings or model versions.
